What affects cost

Four factors can change the final service price.

Hardware type

Standard cylinders, high-security locks and smart locks can require different service work. Grade 1 deadbolts may need specialized tooling, while electronic locks often require programming and compatibility checks. The type and brand of your lock directly influences the parts, tools and time involved in the service.

Access condition

Door alignment, damaged hardware and unusual access conditions can affect the work required. A warped frame, corroded strike plate or previously forced entry can add complexity to the job. Each condition is assessed on-site before confirming the service scope and final pricing.

Timing

Emergency and after-hours service may differ from planned daytime appointments. Late-night or weekend calls often require immediate availability and prioritized dispatch. Scheduling a non-urgent service during regular business hours typically offers the most predictable and economical pricing.

Smart hardware

Connected locks may require hardware-specific installation and configuration. Bluetooth, Wi-Fi and Z-Wave models each have unique setup procedures and compatibility requirements. Pairing with existing smart home systems and testing remote access functionality adds to the overall service time and cost.
